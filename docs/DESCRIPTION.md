## Controls (desktop)

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Move | `W A S D` or `Z Q S D` (menu toggle) |
| Look | Mouse (click the game, or press `F`, to lock the pointer) — arrow keys also strafe/turn |
| Fire / charge rainbow beam | Hold left mouse button or `Space`, release to fire |
| Open doors | `E` or `Enter` when standing in front of one |
| Open Map | `M` |

## Taken. Trapped. Alone.

Creatures of the void have taken you prisoner. Deep in their dungeon you are weakened, and alone. Fight. Run. Escape.

Blast these void spawn with you radiant rainbow beams, take out their leaders, and make you way back to the light... back to freedom.

## About

This is a DDA raycast FPS game inspired by the old greats like Wolfenstein 3D.

Fitting everything into this entry was a challenge, but I hope you enjoy what I managed to squeeze in here!
